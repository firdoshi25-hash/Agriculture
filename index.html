STEP 02

Policy ke instructions dekhein aur insurer, bank, local agriculture office ya official channel se jaldi sampark karein. Reporting deadline rules ke mutabik alag ho sakti hai.

STEP 03

Documents taiyar karein

STEP 04

✓ Claim approval ya payment guaranteed nahi hota.

SAWAL AUR JAWAB

Crop insurance FAQ

Crop insurance ke common sawalon ke detail mein jawab.

Crop insurance ka fayda kya hai?

Crop insurance kuch covered risks se hone wale eligible fasal ke nuksan par financial support de sakta hai. Kaun se risks covered hain, eligibility kya hai aur claim kaise assess hoga—ye aapki policy aur current scheme rules par depend karta hai. Har tarah ka nuksan automatically covered nahi hota.

Kya har kisan aur har fasal eligible hoti hai?

Zaroori nahi. Notified crop, area, season, scheme rules aur policy/enrolment status check karna hota hai. Apne district ki current notification aur official portal, bank ya insurer se eligibility confirm karein.

Fasal ka nuksan hone par sabse pehle kya karun?

Nuksan ki photos/videos, tareekh, location aur crop details sambhal kar rakhein. Apni policy ke instructions dekhein aur relevant insurer ya official channel ko jaldi report karein, kyunki reporting deadlines ho sakti hain. Complaint/reference number note karna na bhoolein.

Claim ke liye kaun se documents chahiye?

Case ke hisaab se policy/application receipt, identity details, bank details, crop/field records, damage evidence aur cultivation/land proof maange ja sakte hain. Ye universal final list nahi hai. Apne claim ke official requirements confirm karein.

Claim kitna milega aur kab milega?

Amount aur timeline policy terms, notified crop/area, loss assessment, required verification aur scheme rules par depend karte hain. Is website ka calculator sirf user-entered example calculate karta hai. Ye official estimate, claim approval ya payment date nahi batata.

Claim reject ya delay ho toh kya karein?

Official channel par status aur rejection/delay ka reason poochhein. Application/reference number, policy details aur pehle ki complaint information sambhal kar rakhein. Policy mein diya grievance process follow karein aur official portal/helpline se current escalation steps confirm karein.

Insurance premium kaise calculate hota hai?

Premium crop, season, location, insured amount aur applicable scheme/policy rules par depend karta hai. Is page ka calculator premium calculate nahi karta. Premium field mein apni policy ya official source se mili amount enter karein.

Kya is website se claim file ho jayega?

Nahi. Ye educational guide hai, official government portal nahi. Claim submit karne, eligibility confirm karne aur latest rules dekhne ke liye official PMFBY portal, insurer, bank ya local agriculture office ka use karein.
